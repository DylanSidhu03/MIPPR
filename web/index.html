MIPPR

Measure and classify hand-picked microplastics (larger than 500 µm) from photos on a printed MIPPR sheet. Everything runs in your browser; photos are not uploaded anywhere.

1. Print a background

Print at actual size (100%), not "fit to page", on matte paper, and check the 100 mm line with a ruler. Place one particle per box, off the lines and out of the box's top-left corner, and photograph from overhead with the whole sheet and all four black corner squares in the frame. Even light, no flash.

    3. Check the results

    Particles the app is unsure about come first. Click a particle, or use the keys: A confirm, X reject, N/P next/previous, C colour, M shape, T tar-like, B fiber bundle.

    4. Download

    One row per particle (particles.csv) and one per sample (summary.csv), as defined in the project's output dictionary. Rejected particles stay in particles.csv and are left out of the summary.

    Visual classification only: whether a particle is plastic, and its polymer type, are not tested.
